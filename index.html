🎓 Flagship Project

AI Study Companion

Problem: Students studying complex material often lack a way to get grounded, trustworthy answers and identify their own weak spots as they learn.

Solution: An agentic AI study assistant — built on a LangGraph ReAct agent with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) — that answers questions grounded in course material, generates quizzes, tracks weak topics, and evaluates its own answer faithfulness.

  • LangGraph-based ReAct agent architecture for multi-step reasoning and tool use
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) grounds answers in real course material, reducing hallucination
  • Automatic quiz generation from studied content
  • Weak-topic tracking to surface knowledge gaps over time
  • LLM-as-judge faithfulness evaluator that scores its own answers for groundedness

MetroVerse AI

Problem: Metro commuters rely on multiple apps for route planning, ticketing, crowd information and station guidance.

Solution: Built a unified AI-powered metro assistant integrating smart routing, ML crowd prediction, conversational AI, live navigation and digital ticketing into one platform.

  • Dijkstra-based shortest path routing across 262 stations on 11 metro lines
  • ML-based crowd prediction model integrated across live routes
  • Groq Llama 3.3 powered AI assistant with session memory
  • QR ticketing, metro card management and real-time journey navigation
  • Push notifications, QR station scanner and nearby tourist guide
